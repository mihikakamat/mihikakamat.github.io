I am a senior at Los Altos High School. Recently, I have been working on research on automated robot design inspired by the biological process of evolution using physics simulators. I have also worked on research on thermophotovoltaic system efficiciency as part of the University of Iowa’s SSTP program.

I am an artist and enjoy drawing and painting in pastels, pencils and acrylic. I also play the keyboard, guitar and the violin, and have been taking soloist training and performing with Notes By the Bay since I was eight.
